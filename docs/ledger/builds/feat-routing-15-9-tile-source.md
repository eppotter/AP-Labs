| #235 | 2026-10-26 | 15.9 | groundwork | T3 | The routing map can now fetch street-map squares from a provider the profile names, ready for Story 15.10 to draw them under the plot. A profile turns it on with an optional `routing.basemap` block (the https address with {z}, {x} and {y}, the credit line and its link, a User-Agent with a contact, zoom, cache days and cache size); every bad value is refused with its key named, and no block means no request. The new fetcher in `kit/runtime/basemap` asks only for the shown view's squares, at most 2 at a time within 4 s each and 8 s a map, takes only small 256 x 256 PNGs, drops the whole layer if one square fails, stops asking for 10 minutes after a refusal, obeys `APLABS_BASEMAP_OFF`, keeps squares in memory only under the provider's cache rules, and never logs or returns an address or square number. It is a T3 engine floor; only the routing-map archetype and the smoke script may use it. Tested against a fake tile server that refuses what OpenStreetMap refuses, with synthetic tiles and no network. No profile turns it on yet (Story 15.11). | DEP-029 |
